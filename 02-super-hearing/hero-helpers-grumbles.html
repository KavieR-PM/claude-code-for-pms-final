What makes the hero helpers grumpy?

Some superheroes have a grown-up helper. The helper watches a big computer screen and makes sure the hero is ready to go.

We talked to 4 helpers. Here's what made them grumpy.

AAmbrose DAunt Dot KKip HHalloran

The phone call runs away too fast

The hero's phone buzzes with a job. But by the time they grab it, the job has zoomed off to another hero!

ADKH 3 out of 4 said this

"by the time he's actually got a thumb on the screen — it's gone. Gone to somebody else already."Aunt Dot

All the beeps sound the same

When a job comes in, every hero's alarm goes "bing!" The helpers can't tell whose it is. Some helpers don't hear it at all.

ADKH 3 out of 4 said this

"right now Mite and Gale both just go "bing" and I have to actually look to know which one."Kip

The words are too tiny to read

The helpers have to squish their noses up to the screen to see anything. And at night, the screen is soooo bright.

ADKH 3 out of 4 said this

"at a glance I sometimes cannot tell engaged from available without leaning in."Ambrose

Some heroes get lots of jobs, some get none

One hero is SO busy and tired. Another hero sits at home all week waiting and waiting. Nobody knows why.

ADKH 2 out of 4 said this

"Mite thinks Mite's been forgotten. Gale's exhausted."Kip

The screen forgets things

The helper sets up the screen just right. Then, poof, it goes back to the way it was, and nobody tells them.

ADKH 1 out of 4 said this

"I'd rather it warned me the filter had reset than simply reset it."Ambrose

New hero gear takes forever

When a hero's armor breaks, the helper asks for a new piece. It waits in a long line with boring stuff like shoelaces.

ADKH 1 out of 4 said this

"One queue, everything in it, doesn't matter if it's laces or plate."Halloran

For the grown-ups: This page summarizes four console-research interviews Sofia Marino ran with Rook Dispatch handlers on 2–5 September 2026. Counts show how many of the four raised each theme. Quotes are verbatim.

The first and fourth grumbles are the Dispatch 4.2 issue: the 60-second offer timeout, and responders who stopped getting callouts. Nobody asked about them; the handlers raised them on their own. The last grumble is a Rook Supply issue. With only four people, treat the counts as a pattern, not a measure of how common each problem is.
