**Personas:** [4–6, grounded in real tickets and interviews, including one who
could lose from the change]

## Neutral questions (never "do you like it?")

1. Tell me about the last time [the situation] happened. Where were you, what were you doing?
2. (show the screen) What do you take from this? What would you do?
3. (next state) What's happening now? What do you expect if you tap?
4. In your own words, what does this mean?
5. What here tells you something you didn't know?
6. Thinking about [the bad period], how, if at all, would this have changed things?
7. What would get in your way or make this less useful? What's missing?
8. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
