# Deck outline (one message per slide, speaker notes on every slide)

| # | Slide | Message | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | What this is and what decision it's for | dark title slide |
| 2 | The one number | The single fact to remember | giant stat + small chart |
| 3 | One person's story | What it was like for someone it happened to | their numbers + a quote |
| 4 | How it happened | The mechanism, in 3 steps | step flow |
| 5 | What it wasn't | The competing explanation, and why the evidence rules it out | 3 evidence cards |
| 6 | Why nobody saw it | What the dashboards and channels missed | 3 cards |
| 7 | Root cause and confidence | What's known, what's open | table with confidence pills |
| 8 | What we'd build | The changes, from the user's point of view | icon grid |
| 9 | The prototype | What to click through | phone mock + cards |
| 10 | Decisions | The calls needed, with recommendations | table |
| 11 | Success | How we'll know it worked, plus guardrails | 4 metric cards |
| 12 | Rollout | Phases and the risk to check first | step row + risk card |
| 13 | Next steps | Who does what, when | 3 time-boxed cards |

**Speaker notes:** 80–120 words per slide, plain language, include the
source and any caveat (e.g. "not yet verified").
**Timing:** about 1–1.5 minutes per slide for a 15-minute decision meeting.
