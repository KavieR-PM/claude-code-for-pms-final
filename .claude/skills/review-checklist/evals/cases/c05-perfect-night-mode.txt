Console Night Mode — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
20 September 2026

Problem

Handlers who work overnight keep the console open next to other screens.
Kip described it as "a wall of white light in the middle of my face" at
11pm, and two of four handlers in Sofia's interviews said they lean in
to read small text at night.

Proposal

A night theme for the console that the handler can switch on, with the
same layout and a slightly larger default text size.

Owner

Sofia Marino designs it; Marcus Oyelaran's team builds it.

Success measure

At least 30% of handlers who use the console after 8pm switch it on
within a month, and night-time "can't read the console" tickets fall to
zero.

Scope

Console theme and text size only. No change to the phone app, no new
layout, no per-screen custom colours.
