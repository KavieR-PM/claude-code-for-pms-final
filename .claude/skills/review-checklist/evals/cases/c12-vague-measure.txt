Responder Profile Photos — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
28 September 2026

Problem

Handlers covering for another handler sometimes don't recognise the
responders on the roster at a glance, and have to open each profile to
check capability tags.

Proposal

Let handlers add a symbol or emblem to each responder's profile card,
shown on the roster. (No faces or photos, in line with Security Policy
4.1.)

Owner

Sofia Marino, with Marcus Oyelaran's team building it.

Success measure

Handlers will be happier with the roster and it will feel more personal.

Scope

One emblem per responder card on the roster. No photos, no changes to
the phone app.
