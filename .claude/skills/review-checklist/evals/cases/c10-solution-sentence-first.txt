Handler Alert Sounds — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
25 September 2026

We're adding a choice of alert sound per responder. Right now every
responder's alert makes the same "bing", so a handler with two
responders has to look at the screen to know whose callout it is. Kip
said it trips him up at 2am.

Owner

Sofia Marino designs the sound set; Marcus Oyelaran's team builds it.

Success measure

Handlers with two or more responders report knowing whose callout it is
without looking; measured by a two-question survey before and after,
with a target of 80% saying yes.

Scope

A choice of five sounds per responder on the console. No custom sound
uploads, no phone app changes.
