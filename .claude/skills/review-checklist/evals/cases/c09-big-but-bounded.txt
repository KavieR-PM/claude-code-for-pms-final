Supply Catalog Search — one-pager
Product, Supply
10 September 2026

Problem

Quartermasters and handlers can't find items unless they know the exact
name. Halloran typed "plate" and got forty results, half unrelated, and
had to scroll to find the vest plate he needed.

Proposal

Rebuild catalog search: category filters, synonyms ("plate" finds "vest
plate"), results ranked by what people actually order, recently ordered
items at the top, and search across all three sites' stock. This is a
large piece of work, and all five parts ship together as one search
experience.

Owner

Halloran's team, Supply, with Ravi Menon on ranking data.

Success measure

The share of searches that end in an order rises from 35% to 60%, and
average time from search to order halves.

Scope

The five search parts above and nothing else. No changes to requisition
approvals, no new catalog items, no mobile app work.
