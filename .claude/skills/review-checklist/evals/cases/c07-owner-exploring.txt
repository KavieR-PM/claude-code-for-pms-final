Responder Availability Calendar — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
15 September 2026

Problem

Handlers set availability one day at a time. Aunt Dot said she re-enters
Vesper's evenings every week because there's no way to repeat them.

Proposal

Let a handler set a repeating weekly availability pattern for a
responder, with one-off exceptions.

Owner

Sofia Marino, exploring this in Q4 alongside the console redesign. Not
yet staffed for a build.

Success measure

Handlers spend less time setting availability: the number of
availability edits per responder per week falls by half.

Scope

Repeating weekly patterns and exceptions only. No shift swapping, no
calendar sync with other tools.
