Offer History Screen — one-pager
Product, Dispatch
2 October 2026

Problem

When an offer times out it disappears from the responder's phone. About
40% of callout tickets ask some version of "is my account broken?"
because responders can't see what they were offered or missed.

Proposal

A "recent offers" screen on the responder app showing the last 30 days
of offers and what happened to each one.

Owner

Wen Li's routing team, with Sofia on the screen design.

Success measure

"Is my account broken?" tickets fall by half. We don't yet have a clean
weekly count of these tickets; Nadia will set one up before launch.

Scope

Read-only history for the responder. No editing, no handler view in this
release.
