CHAPTER 1

WHAT HAPPENED

TOWN NARRATOR

On 12 August, Release 4.2 cut the time a hero has to answer a callout from 90 seconds to 60. That week, every hero missed more jobs.

Dispatch scores a missed job exactly like saying "no", and a miss costs more points than a "yes" earns. Points never come back on their own. So the heroes who missed the most fell to the back of the line, stopped getting offers, and could never earn their way back.

It wasn't a quiet August. And nobody noticed, because the dashboard said things had "recovered".
