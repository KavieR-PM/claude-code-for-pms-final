Week of 31 Aug · same city
Dark mode
Offer context. Every offer leads with what happened, how urgent it is, the drive time and distance from you, a mini map, and what skills it needs, so a responder can decide in seconds.
"You missed a callout." Every missed offer comes back as a flag with its context and one optional tap: away from phone, phone didn’t alert, saw it too late, already on a callout, or too far away. "I’m free now" puts them first in line for the next nearby callout (within a 15-minute drive, for 30 minutes). Handlers see the answers. The answers tell us whether to fix the timer, the notifications or nothing at all.
Addition 2: overload, not just quiet. Mite went from 11 offers to 1 while The Gale went from 13 to 21. Kip sees both, with a reason and an action.
Near-miss grace window. When the 60 seconds run out, the offer stays open for 15 more seconds ("Still open: tap to grab it"), with a second buzz. First tap wins, and the result shows instantly: "It's yours" or "Another responder just took it." Dispatch doesn't slow down, because the next responder is already being asked.
From the hero sessions. A second buzz at 30 seconds, "Accept now, get ready after" on every offer, key info (distance, type) first, and a "You're back on the list" message after the reset.
